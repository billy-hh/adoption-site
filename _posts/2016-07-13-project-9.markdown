description: One hobby that has really grown alongside our family is gardening—especially for Billy! Not long after we bought our home, we built raised garden beds, planted berry bushes and grapevines, and have added to our garden a little more each year. There is something incredibly rewarding about planting a tiny seed, caring for it, and watching it grow. <p> Every summer, we look forward to harvesting blueberries, strawberries, tomatoes, cucumbers, zucchini, grapes, blackberries, and whatever else decides to thrive that season. We almost always end up with more than we can possibly eat, so sharing baskets of fresh produce with our friends, family, and neighbors has become one of our favorite traditions. <p> Now, one of the greatest joys is watching our son experience the garden alongside us. He loves helping us water the plants, searching for ripe berries, and proudly carrying around vegetables that are nearly as big as he is. Seeing the excitement on his face when he discovers something new reminds us that the garden isn't just about growing food anymore—it's about growing curiosity, patience, and an appreciation for the little wonders in everyday life. <p> We can't wait to watch another little set of hands join in, picking tomatoes straight from the vine, tasting sun-warmed blueberries, and making memories together in the backyard that will last a lifetime.
