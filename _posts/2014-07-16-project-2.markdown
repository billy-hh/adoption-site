description: Hi, I'm Billy, and I'm 36 years old. Like Sarah, I was born and raised in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. I graduated from the University of Pittsburgh and now work as a Software Engineering Senior Manager. I enjoy the challenge of solving complex problems and mentoring others, whether that's guiding my team at work or helping our son learn something new, but these days, my favorite title is "Dad."

Becoming our son's dad has been the greatest adventure of my life. There is nothing I enjoy more than seeing the world through his eyes. He's curious about absolutely everything, and I love encouraging that curiosity. Whether we're exploring outside, pushing trucks across the living room floor, splashing in the pool, or figuring out how something works, I love watching him learn and grow. Some of my favorite moments are the simple ones—making him laugh during a game of roughhousing, carrying him on my shoulders, or seeing his face light up when he masters something new.

I've always enjoyed building and creating things. I love gardening, hiking, cooking, and tinkering with just about anything. If something around the house needs to be fixed, I'm usually excited to figure it out. These days, though, I rarely tackle a project alone. Our son proudly grabs his little toy tool bag, follows me wherever I go, and insists on "helping." It may take us twice as long to finish a project, but I wouldn't change a thing. Watching him imitate me and want to learn alongside me is one of the greatest privileges of being his dad.

Watching our son become my little sidekick has been one of the greatest gifts of fatherhood. I smile just thinking about another little helper following us around the house with their own tiny tool bag, eager to join in on whatever we're doing. I can't wait to welcome another child into our family and watch our son step into his new role as a big brother. I know our home has room for another little personality, another curious mind to encourage, and another child to love with my whole heart.

I'll admit I've also had a little fun reliving my own childhood through him. There's something special about introducing him to toy trucks, building sets, and all the things I loved as a kid—and maybe buying a few toys I always wished I'd had myself! Seeing his excitement reminds me just how magical childhood can be.

Family has always been incredibly important to me. I have three siblings, two of whom live nearby, making it easy to get together often. My sister lives in France, which gives us a wonderful excuse to visit family overseas. Whether we're spending time with relatives close to home or making memories while traveling, I love being surrounded by the people I care about most.

I always knew I wanted to be a dad, but becoming one has been even better than I imagined. Watching our son grow has taught me to slow down and appreciate the little moments—the excitement of showing me a rock he found in the yard, the proud smile after he figures something out, or the tiny hand reaching for mine because he wants me to come explore with him. I can't wait to welcome another child into our family, to encourage their curiosity, teach them new things, help them discover what they love, and remind them every single day that they are deeply loved for exactly who they are.
