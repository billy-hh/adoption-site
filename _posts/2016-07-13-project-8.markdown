description: Our home has always been our favorite place, but over the last year and a half, it has become even more special. It's no longer just the place where we live—it has become a home built around the joy, wonder, and laughter of childhood. <p> We have four bedrooms, a big backyard, and plenty of room for family and friends to gather. Most days you'll find our son outside blowing bubbles, digging in the garden, chasing the dogs, or kicking a soccer ball around. Our circular driveway has become the perfect place for little feet to push a bubble lawn mower today and, someday, ride bikes with a little brother or sister. <p> One of our favorite spaces is the playroom. What was once a room we imagined filling with books and toys has become exactly that. The bookshelves are constantly rotating with our latest favorite stories, the toy cabinets are overflowing, and every corner seems to hold another adventure. It's where we build towers, race trucks, dance to silly songs, build forts, and snuggle up with "Big Teddy," our beloved four-foot teddy bear. Our cozy reading nook is one of Sarah's favorite places in the house, and there's nothing better than ending the day together with a stack of books and a sleepy toddler curled up beside us. <p> As much as we treasure the memories we've already made here, we find ourselves dreaming about all the ones still to come. We can picture another little pair of shoes by the door, another favorite bedtime book added to the bookshelf, another tiny voice singing along to silly songs in the playroom, and another curious little helper following us into the garden or kitchen. We smile thinking about two little giggles echoing down the hallway, forts that somehow get even bigger, and bedtime stories with one child snuggled on each side of us. <p> If you asked our friends and family what our home feels like, we hope they'd tell you there's always enough food, enough laughter, and always room for one more around the table. More than anything, we hope our home is a place where our children always feel safe, celebrated, and completely themselves. A place where they know they are deeply loved, where traditions are made, where scraped knees are kissed, big feelings are welcomed, bedtime stories are never rushed, and ordinary Tuesday nights can feel just as magical as Christmas morning. <p> Our greatest hope is that, from the moment they arrive, they will always know this is their home, they belong here, and they are loved beyond measure.
