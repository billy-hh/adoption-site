Our home has always been our favorite place, but over the last year and a half, it's become even more special. It's no longer just a house—it truly feels like a home built around the joy of childhood.

We have four bedrooms, plenty of space for family and friends to visit, and a big backyard where our son loves to run, explore, and burn off some toddler energy. Whether we're blowing bubbles, playing fetch with the dogs, digging in the garden, or kicking a soccer ball around, there's always something to do outside. Our front yard has a circular driveway that has quickly become the perfect place for little feet to push his toy bubble lawn mower today and, someday, ride bikes with a sibling.

One of our favorite spaces is our playroom. What was once a room we dreamed would one day be filled with books and toys is now exactly that. My favorite zone is our cozy reading nook - lined with our favorite stories that get read over and over again, cabinets are overflowing with toys, and every corner seems to hold another little adventure waiting to happen. It's where we build towers, race cars, dance to silly songs, make forts and snuggle up with "big teddy" our resident 4 foot teddy bear. Watching our son play, learn, and laugh in this room has been one of the greatest joys of parenthood.

Our home isn't perfectly tidy anymore—and honestly, we wouldn't have it any other way. It's filled with tiny shoes by the door, magnetic alphabet letters on the refrigerator, toys that somehow find their way into every room, and the kind of joyful chaos that comes with raising a happy little boy. We cannot wait to watch another child make this house even more lively, filling it with more laughter, more imagination, and more memories. There is more than enough room in our hearts and in our home for another little one to be loved, celebrated, and completely at home here.
