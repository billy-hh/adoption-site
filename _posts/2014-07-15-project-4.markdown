description: Toby is our 8-year-old rescue pup and the gentler of our two dogs. We adopted him in the late summer of 2018, and he quickly settled into our family as the sweetest, most easygoing companion. His rescue organization believes he is part lab, part hound, and mostly just very cute—which feels very accurate to us. <p>Toby has occasional bursts of energy where he loves to zoom around the house and yard, but most of the time he is happiest simply being near his people. He especially loves kids and has become very attached to our son. He is known for his “drive-by kisses,” gently stopping by for a quick hello before going about his day. <p>When he’s not curled up in his favorite chair by the window or patiently sitting under the high chair waiting for the occasional dropped bite, you can usually find him outside sunbathing in the yard. He brings a calm, steady presence to our home and is very patient with all the noise, toys, and excitement that come with life with a toddler.
