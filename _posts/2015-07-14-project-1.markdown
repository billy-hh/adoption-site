description: Watson is our 9-year-old rescue pup and an important member of our family. We brought him home a few months after we got engaged, during the summer we moved into our first house. He got to grow up alongside us in a backyard that was perfect for a dog to run, explore, and relax—just like the one we have now. Watson’s rescue organization believes he is part Labrador, part hound, and part heeler, which feels very accurate in his personality!<p> He is always hungry, incredibly smart, and very motivated by treats. He knows a wide variety of tricks and is more than happy to show them off as many times as you would like, as long as there’s a reward involved. These days, Watson has embraced two of his favorite jobs in the family - toy inspector and official high chair cleanup crew.  He patiently waits nearby during mealtimes, ready to catch any little bits that might make their way to the floor.
