Hi, I'm Sarah, and I'm 37 years old. I was born and raised in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and after graduating from Penn State's main campus, I built a career as a Software Engineer. I genuinely enjoy solving problems, building things, and the challenge my work brings, but my most important and favorite role is being a mom.

Over a year and a half ago, Billy and I welcomed our incredible son through adoption, and becoming his mom has been the greatest joy of my life. He is the sweetest, happiest, most energetic, and endlessly curious little boy. Watching him discover the world has changed me in ways I never imagined. Every day is filled with laughter, hugs, new adventures, and countless little moments that remind me just how lucky I am. Motherhood has given my life a deeper purpose, and there is nothing I love more than being his safe place, biggest cheerleader, and favorite person to explore life with.

When I'm not chasing after or on the floor playing with our little guy, you'll usually find me in the kitchen trying a new recipe, curled up with a good book, or enjoying a little reality TV after bedtime. Billy and I love cooking together and are always looking for new restaurants, recipes, and foods to try. One of my newest passions is creating fun, healthy meals for our son—especially if I can sneak a few extra veggies into them! Some of my happiest memories are gathered around a table with family, sharing a meal, laughing together, and simply enjoying each other's company.

Family has always been the center of my life. I have three amazing siblings, each with wonderful families of their own, and I grew up surrounded by a huge Greek and Italian family. My mom is the youngest of eight siblings, and my dad is the youngest of seven, so family gatherings are wonderfully loud, full of laughter, and always centered around good food and even better company. Our son is growing up surrounded by grandparents, aunts, uncles, cousins, and so many people who absolutely adore him, and we can't wait to welcome another child into that same circle of unconditional love.

Every day of being our son's mom has made my heart grow even bigger than I ever thought possible. The love I have for him is unlike anything I've ever experienced. And somehow, I know there's still so much room in my heart to love another child just as deeply. I cannot wait to welcome another little one into our family and give our son the incredible gift of a sibling. I dream about watching him become a big brother—sharing toys (most of the time!), reading books together, building forts in the playroom, cheering each other on at soccer games, and creating the kind of lifelong sibling bond that becomes one of life's greatest gifts.

I'm also excited to discover who this new little soul will be. I can't wait to learn what makes them laugh, what sparks their curiosity, what brings them comfort, and what makes them uniquely them. I promise to be their biggest cheerleader, to love every single thing that makes them who they are, and to spend every day helping them grow into the happiest, kindest, and most confident version of themselves. And just as importantly, I will always honor the love that brought them into our family and the dreams you have for their future.

Our home is filled with love, laughter, curiosity, and encouragement. We believe in celebrating the little moments just as much as the big milestones. We read bedtime stories every night, dance in the kitchen, spend time outside, and make ordinary days feel special. If we are fortunate enough to grow our family through adoption again, I promise your child will be loved beyond measure, celebrated for exactly who they are, and surrounded by a family that will cherish them every single day.

Adoption has been the greatest blessing in our lives, and we are forever grateful for our son's incredible birth parents, who entrusted us with the privilege of becoming his parents. They are now an extension of our family, and not a day goes by that we don't appreciate the extraordinary gift they gave us. As we hope to grow our family once more, we carry that same gratitude in our hearts and look forward to the possibility of sharing our love with another child.
