description: Hi, I'm Sarah, and I'm 37 years old. I was born and raised in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and after graduating from Penn State's main campus, I built a career as a Software Engineer. I genuinely enjoy solving problems, building things, and the challenge my work brings, but my most important and favorite role is being a mom. <p> Over a year and a half ago, Billy and I welcomed our incredible son through adoption, and becoming his mom has been the greatest joy of my life. He is the sweetest, happiest, most energetic, and endlessly curious little boy. Watching him discover the world has changed me in ways I never imagined. Every day is filled with laughter, hugs, new adventures, and countless little moments that remind me just how lucky I am. Motherhood has given my life a deeper purpose, and there is nothing I love more than being his safe place, biggest cheerleader, and favorite person to explore life with. <p> When I'm not chasing after or on the floor playing with our little guy, you'll usually find me in the kitchen trying a new recipe, curled up with a good book, or enjoying a little reality TV after bedtime. Billy and I love cooking together and are always looking for new restaurants, recipes, and foods to try. One of my newest passions is creating fun, healthy meals for our son—especially if I can sneak a few extra veggies into them! Some of my happiest memories are gathered around a table with family, sharing a meal, laughing together, and simply enjoying each other's company. <p> I also weirdly love to tidy up. I have a bit of a passion for creating a home that feels warm, peaceful, and welcoming. I genuinely enjoy decorating and finding little ways to make our house feel extra special for our family. There is something so rewarding about creating a place where the people I love feel safe, comfortable, and completely at home. I love the feeling of home so being able to create that safe space for our family is such an honor. <p> Family has always been the center of my life. I have three amazing siblings, each with wonderful families of their own, and I grew up surrounded by a huge Greek and Italian family. My mom is the youngest of eight siblings, and my dad is the youngest of seven, so family gatherings are wonderfully loud, full of laughter, and always centered around good food and even better company. Our son is growing up surrounded by grandparents, aunts, uncles, cousins, and so many people who absolutely adore him, and we can't wait to welcome another child into that same circle of unconditional love. <p> I lost both of my parents in my late twenties, just a short time apart. During one of the hardest seasons of my life, my siblings became my greatest source of strength, and it showed me just how extraordinary that bond can be. More than anything, I hope our son and a future child have the opportunity to grow up with that same kind of lifelong teammate, someone to celebrate life's happiest moments with, lean on through its hardest ones, and always call family. <p> I am so excited to meet our future child and discover everything that makes them uniquely them. I can't wait to learn what comforts them after a hard moment, what sparks their curiosity, what foods they'll ask for again and again, which bedtime story they'll insist on reading "just one more time," and all of the little quirks that make them who they are. Every day of being a mom has shown me that love doesn't have limits—it simply grows. My greatest hope is to help them grow into the happiest, kindest, and most confident version of themselves while giving them a childhood filled with security, wonder, laughter, and unconditional love.
