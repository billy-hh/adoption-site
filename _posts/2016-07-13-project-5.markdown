description: Travel has always been one of our favorite ways to spend time together. Whether it's exploring a new country, taking a weekend road trip to see family, or returning to our favorite family traditions, we love experiencing new places and making memories along the way.

Together, we've traveled to six countries, visited countless states, and every summer we look forward to our annual family vacation to Hilton Head. Last year was especially memorable because we experienced it through our son's eyes for the very first time. He absolutely loved splashing in the pool between naptimes...the sand, on the other hand, was a little less impressive! We're excited to see what this year's trip has in store now that he's running everywhere and eager to explore.

One adventure we're especially looking forward to is getting our son's first passport and taking him to Europe to meet extended family and experience the places that are so meaningful to us. We can't wait to watch him discover new cultures, foods, languages, and traditions while creating memories that will stay with our family for years to come.

Wherever we go, our favorite part of traveling isn't checking destinations off a list—it's being together. We look forward to sharing that same sense of adventure with another child, helping them discover the world while always knowing that home is wherever we're together.
