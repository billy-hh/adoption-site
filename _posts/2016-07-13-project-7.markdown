description: One of our favorite things to do is bring people together. Our home has become the place where family and friends gather to celebrate holidays, milestones, and even the ordinary moments that are made special simply because we're together.

Each year our get-togethers become a little bigger and a little livelier. One of our favorite traditions is hosting a New Year's Eve celebration, complete with shucking fresh oysters for our family and friends. This past year, though, our celebration looked a little different. Instead of staying up until midnight, we hosted a "pre-bedtime" New Year's Eve party with some of our closest friends and all of our little ones. Watching the toddlers run around together in their pajamas was every bit as fun as the late-night celebrations of years past—and honestly, we wouldn't have traded it for anything.

We also love hosting Easter, Thanksgiving, and Christmas, filling our home with good food, laughter, and the people we love most. The highlight of our year is our annual Oktoberfest, which has become our biggest tradition. We make homemade potato pancakes (using potatoes from our garden whenever we're lucky enough!), enjoy great food and music, and spend the day surrounded by the people who mean the most to us.

Now that we're parents, these traditions have taken on an entirely new meaning. Seeing the holidays through our son's eyes has made every celebration more magical—from watching him hunt for Easter eggs with his cousins, to seeing his excitement on Christmas morning. We can't wait to experience those moments all over again with another child and to watch our son share these traditions with a little brother or sister. More than anything, we hope our home will always be the place where our children know they are deeply loved, where everyone is welcome, and where some of their favorite memories are made around a table surrounded by family and friends.
