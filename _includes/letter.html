Thank You


Dear Birth Family,

Thank you for taking the time to learn about our family. We know that this is a deeply personal and incredibly meaningful decision, and we do not take it lightly that you are here, considering us as part of your journey. We are truly grateful for the opportunity to share our story with you.

We are Sarah and Billy, and we live right outside of Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Our lives are full in the most beautiful way—centered around love, family, and the joyful chaos of raising our son, whom we welcomed through adoption a year and a half ago. Becoming parents has changed everything for us. It has deepened who we are, how we love, and how we see the world. Our son is funny, curious, energetic, and endlessly loved, and every single day with him feels like both a gift and a reminder of how much we have to be grateful for.

Watching him grow has been the greatest joy of our lives. Sarah is the one he runs to for comfort, laughter, and everyday adventures, and she delights in discovering who he is becoming moment by moment. Billy is his constant sidekick—following him through the yard, building things together, fixing whatever needs fixing, and encouraging his curiosity with endless patience and excitement. Together, we’ve found a rhythm as a family that fills our home with laughter, movement, and love.

Our home is lively in the best way. It is filled with books, toys, tiny shoes, and the kind of happy mess that comes with a toddler exploring everything for the first time. Our playroom is constantly in use, our backyard is a place of discovery, and even the kitchen has become a space for little helpers and new traditions. We cook together often, experiment with new recipes, and love sharing meals with family—especially Sarah’s large Greek and Italian family, where gatherings are loud, warm, and full of connection. Billy’s family is equally close, and between both sides, our son is surrounded by grandparents, cousins, aunts, and uncles who adore him deeply.

We are also incredibly lucky to have two dogs, Watson and Toby, who are very much part of our family. They bring both energy and calm into our home—one always ready for play, the other always ready for a nap in the sun—and they are wonderfully patient and gentle with our son.

As parents, we’ve learned that the most meaningful moments are often the smallest ones. Morning cuddles, messy meals, bedtime routines, watching our son proudly show us something he’s discovered—these are the moments that have shaped our hearts. We’ve learned to slow down, to notice more, and to truly delight in the person our child is becoming.

And through all of this, one truth has become even clearer to us: our hearts have more room to love.

We feel so deeply grateful for the adoption that made us parents, and for the incredible birth parents who entrusted us with that gift. We carry that gratitude with us every day, and we understand that adoption is built on both love and courage. If you choose us, we promise to honor your child’s story with honesty, respect, and deep appreciation for the role you play in their life.

We would be honored beyond words to grow our family again through adoption. We dream of our son becoming a big brother—of him sharing his toys, leading the way one moment and learning to share the next, and growing up alongside a sibling who will be his lifelong companion. We also look forward to discovering another little personality, another unique spirit to know and love completely, just as we do with our son.

Above all, we promise a home filled with love, stability, laughter, and encouragement. A home where your child will be celebrated for exactly who they are, where they will always feel safe and deeply loved, and where they will grow up surrounded by family who adores them.

Thank you again for considering us. We are truly honored to be part of your search, and we would be grateful for the opportunity to get to know you and share more about our lives.

With heartfelt gratitude,

Billy & Sarah
