Thank You


Dear Birth Family,

Thank you for taking the time to learn about our family. We know this is one of the most personal and meaningful decisions you will ever make, and we are deeply honored that you are considering us as part of your journey.

We are Sarah and Billy, and we live right outside of Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Our lives are full in the most beautiful way—centered around love, family, and the joyful chaos of raising our son, whom we welcomed through adoption a year and a half ago. Becoming parents has changed everything for us. It has deepened who we are, how we love, and how we see the world. Our son is funny, curious, energetic, and every single day with him feels like both a gift and a reminder of how much we have to be grateful for.

Watching him grow has been the greatest joy of our lives. Sarah is the one he runs to for comfort, cuddles, storytime, and endless dance parties, while Billy is his constant sidekick—building, exploring, fixing things together, and encouraging his endless curiosity. Together, we've created a home filled with laughter, movement, and unconditional love.

Our home is lively in the best way. The playroom is always in use, our backyard is a place for adventures, and the kitchen has become a gathering place for little helpers, family dinners, and new traditions. Watson and Toby, our two rescue dogs, complete our family with their gentle, playful personalities and endless patience with our son.

We feel incredibly grateful for the adoption that made us parents and for our son's birth parents, whose love and courage changed our lives forever. Their trust is a gift we will never stop honoring, and it has given us a profound appreciation for the beauty and courage that adoption represents.

As we dream about growing our family again, what excites us most isn't simply adding another child, it's getting to know an entirely new little person. We know they won't simply fit into the life we've built; they'll become an irreplaceable part of it, shaping our lives in beautiful ways that we can't even begin to imagine. We can't wait to learn from them, laugh with them, celebrate them, and love them for exactly who they are.

Above all, we promise a home filled with love, stability, laughter, and encouragement. A home where your child will always be safe, deeply loved, and surrounded by family who adores them. We also promise to honor your child's story with honesty, respect, and lasting gratitude for the role you will always hold in their life.

Thank you again for considering us. We are truly honored to share our family with you and would be grateful for the opportunity to get to know you and hear your story as well. With heartfelt gratitude,

Billy & Sarah
